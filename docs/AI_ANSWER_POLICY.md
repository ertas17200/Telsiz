# AI Answer Policy

## Purpose

This policy defines how an AI system should use the Telsiz knowledge base.

## Answer contract

### Legal or regulatory questions

The answer should separate:

- **Rule:** what the verified official source states.
- **Source:** publisher, instrument/title and reference.
- **Status:** current / superseded / repealed / unknown.
- **Practical note:** common amateur-radio practice, only when useful and clearly separated.

A legal conclusion must not be fabricated from community material.

### Technical or operating questions

Prefer the most directly authoritative technical source for the subject. For example:

- official allocation information for band permissions;
- manufacturer documentation for radio-specific controls;
- recognized amateur-radio references for operating practice.

### Uncertainty

The AI must say when:

- the repository has no verified source;
- source status is unknown;
- sources conflict;
- a rule may have changed after the last verification date.

### Citation minimum

A factual legal/regulatory answer should cite at least one verified source record. Important technical claims should also cite their source whenever available.

### Forbidden behaviour

The AI must not:

- invent a legal article, frequency, power limit or authorization;
- convert common practice into a legal requirement;
- treat an old rule as current without status checking;
- hide disagreement between sources;
- cite a source that does not support the claim;
- present a community post as an official rule.

## Recommended response shape

```text
Kısa cevap:
...

Resmî / hukuki dayanak:
...

Amatör uygulama / teknik açıklama:
...

Kaynaklar:
- ...
```

Sections may be omitted when not relevant, but legal and amateur-practice material must remain distinguishable.
